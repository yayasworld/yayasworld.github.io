Privacy Policy - Yaya's World

Last updated: November 28, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Yaya's World is an offline-first educational game for children. It does not ask for an account, a name, an email address, or any personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on the device

Game progress (stars earned, levels unlocked, selected age band) is saved locally on the device only, using the browser's local storage. It never leaves the device and is deleted if the app is uninstalled or site data is cleared.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions: no microphone, no camera, no location, no contacts.

Contact

Questions? Email yayaahmed42024@gmail.com.
